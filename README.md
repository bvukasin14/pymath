Pymath is made for math projects in Python, and the module is also coded in Python, so its easy to understand what the module does and easy to make your version.

--------
**Functions**
--------
Perimeter

  The perimeter class does what it says - calculates perimeter! You just choose what you want - cirle, square, rectangle - and add the values needed.
  
  *Example:*
    
    print(pymath.perimeter.square(3))
 
  *Output:*

    12
    
Area

  Same as perimeter class, but just for area calculation.
  
  *Example*
   
    print(pymath.area.square(3))
  
  *Output:*
  
    9
    
Convert

  For me the most exciting function - converter. It can convert size, area and volume. You just provide value and two units of measurement and watch the magic behind it!
  
  *Example:*
  
    print(pymath.convert.size(3, "m", "cm"))
  *Output:*
  
    300
    
Squared

  It is just simple powering.
  
  *Example:*
  
    print(pymath.squared(3, 2))
  
  *Output:*
  
    9
    
Abs

  Abs stands for absolute value.

  *Example:*
  
    print(pymath.abs(-3))
  
  *Output:*
   
    3
